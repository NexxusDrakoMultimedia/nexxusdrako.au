About me

I’ve been gaming since I was three. I’ve barely stopped since.

Where it started

My first game was Crash Bandicoot on the original PlayStation. I died constantly. At three, I didn’t really get what dying meant, so I kept going.

Over the next few years the house filled up with games, then a Nintendo 64, then a PlayStation 2. When my parents split, most of it left with Dad. For a while my whole gaming world was one cartridge: Pokémon Ruby. I beat it in 14 hours at eight, and finished the Hoenn Pokédex by 66 hours, with a bit of trading help.

The PS2 years

In 2004 I got another PS2. I still own it. I played it daily for about eight years: every Need for Speed, fifty-odd other games, and my favourite game of all time, Let’s Make A Soccer Team!. A Wii turned up for Christmas 2010 and was packed away about as fast as it was unwrapped. The PS2 was king.

Console hopping

In 2012 I sold the Wii, my DS, 13 DS games and a stack of older titles to buy a PS3. A year later, on my 17th birthday, I traded it in for an Xbox 360. I liked it more, Xbox Live fees and all.

In June 2015 I rent-to-bought a top-end gaming laptop, my first PC that wasn’t terrible. The 360 and a fairly new 3DS gathered dust almost straight away, and selling them paid for accessories, games and an Xbox One.

Going full PC

In 2019 I did a Certificate III in IT (Networking) at TAFE NSW Albury, and won Business and Technology Student of the Year. That same year I built my first PC from parts and sold the Xbox One. I’ve been PC-first ever since, building and maintaining my own rigs.

The consoles never fully left, though. These days they’re for retro and handheld play: a modded PS2, a Wii, a Switch and a 3DS.

Making things

I’ve always pulled games apart as much as I’ve played them. It started with editing text files in Bygfoot and CART Precision Racing to add my own drivers and teams. In grade 5, at 11, I made my first real game in Game Maker, a top-down maze game called Adventures of the Fat Guy. A lot of Game Maker and RPG Maker projects came after it.

Since then:

  • Kitsu’s Destiny: an incremental, legacy-style RPG made in RPG Maker MZ. It came out on Steam in late 2022 and has sold over 1,500 copies.
  • LMASTER Mod and LMAST Realism Mod: bug fixes, restored features, and real teams and logos for Let’s Make A Soccer Team!.
  • Motorsport Manager Revamped: a vanilla-plus overhaul, made with Ohayoghurt and Lonewolf Commander.
  • Assetto Corsa tracks: including the Nexxus Drako Autodrome, built from a sketch I drew about 15 years ago, plus circuits set around Healesville and Albury-Wodonga.
  • Bits and pieces: Football Manager fantasy leagues, Kerbal Space Program mods, cricket sims, board games, and a few contributions to the PCSX2 emulator.

Mostly I just like messing around in new tools and seeing what comes out.

Streaming

I started streaming in 2018 and stopped after COVID. In early 2024 I came back with a fresh outlook after a rough stretch with my mental health. On stream it’s mostly Let’s Make A Soccer Team!, sims, strategy, sport and racing, usually as my VTuber avatar. I’m taking a break at the moment, but the channel isn’t going anywhere.

Alongside Ohayoghurt, I’ve kept the LMAST community going for years. It’s been just the two of us keeping the fire lit for a game most people forgot.

Who I am

I’m an autistic sapphic furry and a plural demigirl, and I’m transfem, nonbinary and queer. My pronouns are she/her and they/them.

I started questioning my gender in 2013 and came out as nonbinary in 2019. I moved to Brisbane in 2021 and started my legal and medical transition there.

Trans rights are human rights.

The mythos

Everything I make belongs to one personal mythos: the games, the tracks, the music, the Itavrnai. Nexxus Drako sits at the centre of it, and that’s me.
