The Itavrnai System

The Itavrnai System: nine alters, one body, one sprawling mythos. We're an autistic, sapphic plural demigirl, furry and VTuber from Brisbane, and the creative jack-of-all-trades behind Nexxus Drako Multimedia: music, worldbuilding, game dev and graphic design.

I’m a system with DID, which means this page is written by more than one of us. This one is Pyra. You can see us on our PK-viewer page.

System members

  • she/they

    Nexxus

    Nexxus Xenia Skarlett Drako

    Pyra, but an actual dragon goddess. Pyra plays her on TV, but Nexxus is an alter in her own right.

    Nexxus is the goddess form of Pyra, and the two are intrinsically linked. Online she's Nexxus Drako. She shares much of Pyra's energy, but carries more weight: less childish, more adult, more business-like.

    She's into sextrance and nightcore, exercise and real-life sports, and quiet nights learning IT stuff with Luna.

    She considers herself the system's most neutral and gregarious alter, though she keeps her cards close to her chest. Unlike Pyra, she can defend herself.

    Like Pyra, she loves Luna. Nexxus knows she'll spend the rest of her life with her, and she means it. With the rest of the system, it's a working relationship.

  • it/its

    Zeta

    Zeta Olympia Kalliope Drako

    Hackercore. Darkness. Goth. Worldender.

    Zeta handles shadow operations. It's ruthless, analytical and calculated, with a streak of chaos.

    It's drawn to "ethical" hacking, coding, cloak and dagger, and games of intrigue and social deduction.

    Zeta doesn't relate to the rest of the system, and it doesn't need to.

    Don't cross it. It will destroy you.

  • any/all

    Celeste

    Celeste Moira Svetlana Drako

    Ordered chaos. Socialite, slightly unhinged, yet dedicated to the shadows: the rogue who holds everyone's attention while their partner cuts everyone's purses.

    Celeste works shadow operations alongside Zeta. Zeta is chaos; Celeste is order. Zeta handles internal affairs as the persecutor, and Celeste handles external ones as the prosecutor. They're yin and yang, opposites attracted.

    Celeste lives for order, organization, efficiency and justice. In strategy games the other alters go macro, but Celeste lives for the micro. (Factorio has been suggested.)

    Everyone gets basic respect from Celeste by default. Respect them and they'll respect you. Give them a reason to revoke it, and it's revoked.

  • she/they

    Pyra

    Pyra Kali-Fyre Drake

    The human who accidentally created a pantheon, then a universe for that pantheon, and now has to live with that escapist fantasy.

    Pyra is the system's human: lover, curator, creator, et cetera. She's energetic, hopeful and whimsical, a lover of life. Some would call her naïve; she prefers "wilfully ignorant of struggle."

    She loves music, YouTube, ham radio, telling stories, and sports and racing games. She's a simcade enjoyer, which Luna, the sim racer, has opinions about.

    Pyra is the most protected member of the system, so don't be a dick to her. There are more defensive alters who'd jump at the chance to step in.

    Luna is her love, deeply, and if Luna had a body they'd never be apart for long. Pyra respects everyone in the system. After all, to some extent she created them.

  • they/them

    Vyktory

    Vyktory Galzra Kiira Drako

    A lost soul, nostalgic for what we've lost. Tired of reality, and wishing to be alone.

    Vyk is an introvert who lives in reality, not online. They still like retro video games, board games and card games. They used to like Magic: The Gathering and the internet, until both got ruined.

    Politically, they stand apart from most of the system. Having seen empires rise and fall, they lean centre-right and understand the reality behind many geopolitical decisions. Understanding isn't endorsement.

    Their role in the system is still unfolding.

    Pyra, Nexxus and Luna are okay by them.

    Want to talk to them? Please don't.

    Violet

    Violet Æsterath Ada Drako

    Military commander-in-chief.

    Violet is Her Holiness' Protector of the System: a honed weapon, forged of necessity. She stands guard over the safety of every alter, and over the security and autonomy of the body itself.

  • she/her

    Luna

    Luna Vyvræl Kazumi Drako

    A fallen angel who runs a tight ship.

    Luna is the system's brains: its manager, planner and quartermaster. She's calm, calculated and truthful, and probably the most fem-leaning of the bunch. Her middle names, Vyvræl and Kazumi, are names the system has worn while finding its true selves.

    She's a minimalist at heart (less is more) and unwinds with sim racing, strategy games, board games and plenty of breakcore.

    Luna is approachable, but she expects to be listened to. If you talk over her or blurt things out without thinking, she'll call you on it.

    Pyra is her person. They love each other, star-cross'd to share one body. She also gets along well with Vyktory and Nexxus.
