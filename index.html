What I make

Projects

Here is a portfolio of the major projects I've been working on these past 15 years or so.

  • Stream

    Nexxus Drako Multimedia

    My Twitch, YouTube, and Discord server are all part of the Nexxus Drako Multimedia umbrella as I create a cohesive VTuber personality and brand.

    Watch on Twitch
  • Education

    Qualifications

    I have a Certificate III in Information Technology, specialising in Networking, attained in 2019 from TAFE NSW's Albury campus, where I also won TAFE NSW Riverina Business and Technology Student of the Year for helping my fellow classmates after I'd finished coursework ahead of schedule.

  • Radio

    Ham radio

    I've been ham radio licensed under callsign VK4PKD since April 6th, 2025. I'm still merely dabbling in the hobby using a VHF handheld and listening on the local repeaters, I have the intent to eventually get a HF rig though.

  • Game dev

    Kitsu's Destiny

    Kitsu's Destiny is an RPG Maker MZ game I made which focuses heavily on incremental, legacy-style gameplay. It's sold over 1,500 units since releasing in late 2022. It is available on Steam, and soon on Android.

    Buy on Steam
  • Board games

    Board game design

    I design tabletop games too. Cunning is a trick-taking game where everyone plays face down and the best card takes the points, now in playtesting on Tabletop Simulator. Conquest Metagame is a map war where every invasion is settled by a randomly chosen board game, and King and Ace Cricket plays a whole cricket match with a standard deck of cards.

    See my designs on BoardGameGeek
  • Music

    Extrasolar Detonation

    In 2018 I started an EDM project called Extrasolar Detonation. Some of my songs were regional/rural Australian number ones in the weekly independent Atomic Music charts plus two top 20s in the 2019 Atomic 100.

    Buy on Bandcamp
  • Music

    Itavrnai Protocol

    In 2025, I started a reboot and follow-up to Extrasolar Detonation called Itavrnai Protocol, whose first album, Genesys, released in early 2026. It also had solid runs in the Atomic Music charts and has also been played on 4ZZZ Community Radio in Brisbane.

    Listen to Genesys
  • Mod

    LMAST Realism Mod

    I created a mod for the game Let's Make A Soccer Team! replacing unlicensed team logos with real ones, as well as competition logos, with a kit and UI overhaul in the research phase.

    Download the mod
  • Mod

    LMASTER Mod

    A fix-and-restore mod for Let's Make a Soccer Team! on PlayStation 2 (European release). It fixes bugs the game shipped with and brings back features that were switched off, and its Full and Max editions add a hidden third tier and the real names and grounds of the 2005-06 season.

    Get it on GitHub
  • Mod

    Motorsport Manager Revamped

    A mod for Motorsport Manager combining my work with the work of MM modding stalwarts Ohayoghurt and Lonewolf Commander to create a vanilla-plus revamp of the game and its mechanics.

    Download the mod
  • Worldbuilding

    Itavran Union

    A worldbuilding project within a private shared universe, the Union is a space-faring nation with five species from five different planets in the Itavrnai system.

  • Geofiction

    NationStates & geofiction

    I've been roleplaying sports and various other things on NationStates on and off for 15 years, while also working on collaborative geofiction as part of the Micras Sector. The main nation I'm currently working on is New Bradman, while my previous nation was the Commonwealth of Vyktory.
