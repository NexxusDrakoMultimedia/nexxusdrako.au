Board games

I'm a board game designer in Brisbane. These are the tabletop games I've designed.

See my designs on BoardGameGeek

Cunning

Cunning is a trick-taking game where everyone plays a card face down at the same time, and the best card wins points equal to the value of every card in play. Each player is dealt seven cards and plays seven tricks a round, and before each round the table can declare whether the high or low card is trumps.

It's currently being playtested online on Tabletop Simulator, and there is no physical release yet. Cunning was designed by Pyra Drake and published by Nexxus Drako Multimedia.

The current cards were generated with code written by Claude Sonnet 5 and will be replaced with playing-card art for a limited physical release.

Conquest Metagame

Conquest Metagame is a map-conquest game for two to eight players where every invasion is settled by a board game picked on a wheel. It's played on a fictional balkanized US map with 100 territories, and a host spins the wheel and referees.

The rules and map are on GitHub under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Read the rules on GitHub

King and Ace Cricket

King and Ace Cricket plays a whole cricket match with a standard 52-card deck: six balls an over, a six-card hand per side, the higher card wins and a King is a wicket.

There's a web app at kac.nexxusdrako.au for solo innings, solo 1v1 matches and tournaments, and the rules are released under Creative Commons Zero.
